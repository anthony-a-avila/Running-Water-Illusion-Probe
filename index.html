"Running Water" Illusion Probes

About

Self-test rounds for the post-tone "running water" effect heard during sustained tone listening. Each round plays tone → noise sequences. Rate each one perceived, boundary, or not perceived.

Headphones recommended. Keep volume low. Sawtooth and square at high gain hurt.

9 presets. Waveform, frequency, and duration probes to find a rough boundary.
